## Rehearsing with Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs like Claude or GPT can serve as effective roleplay partners for mock decomposition sessions.

> [!TIP]
> When using LLMs for practice, prompt them to act as a **demanding enterprise customer stakeholder**. Ask the model to hold back details until you ask specific clarifying questions, and request feedback on whether your proposed solutions account for real-world enterprise constraints (compliance, legacy systems, latency, and operational cost).
